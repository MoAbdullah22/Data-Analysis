Final Executive Questions

After all of this analysis, the final dashboard should answer approximately these 10 questions:

01 — Business Health

How is DataCo's overall business performing over time?

02 — Growth

Is the business growing in sales, orders, customers, and profitability?

03 — Profitability

Where is the company generating strong profit, and where is profitability weak?

04 — Products

Which products and categories are the major sales and profit drivers?

05 — Pricing

Are discounts supporting sales growth or putting pressure on profitability?

06 — Delivery

How significant is the late-delivery problem, and how is it changing?

07 — Logistics

08 — Customers

Which customer segments generate the greatest commercial value?

09 — Markets

Which markets are strongest financially and which require attention?

10 — Strategic Priorities

What are the most important areas where management should take action?
